How to read the indicators

RSI (Relative Strength Index, 14 days)
A 0–100 score of how strongly the price has moved up vs down lately. Above 70 = "overbought" (risen fast, may cool off); below 30 = "oversold" (fallen fast, may bounce). In strong trends it can stay extreme for a long time.
MACD (12, 26, 9)
The gap between a fast (12-day) and slow (26-day) average of the price. When the MACD line crosses above its 9-day signal line, momentum is turning up; crossing below, turning down. The bars (histogram) show the distance between the two.
Bollinger Bands (20 days, 2 standard deviations)
A band around the 20-day average that widens when the price is jumpy and narrows when it's calm. Price near the upper band = high vs its recent range; near the lower band = low. %B puts this on a scale: 0 = lower band, 1 = upper band. A "squeeze" (very narrow bands) often comes before a bigger move.
200-day average
Used as the long-term trend filter: price above it = uptrend, below = downtrend.
Past record
For every time a setup appeared in the test period, we check whether the stock was higher 21 trading days (~1 month) later. "All stocks" is the same check on every stock and every day, so you can see whether the setup actually added anything. It only uses today's S&P 500 members (so it ignores companies that dropped out) and doesn't include fees.
